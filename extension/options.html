Mydroid Authenticator

Fill 2FA codes from your Android phone.

How it works

  1. Click Fill from phone next to a code field.
  2. Approve on your phone.
  3. New site? Link… it to an account. Lookalike sites get a warning.

Set up

Mydroid › Settings › Authenticator › Set Up

Privacy

Secrets never leave your phone. Nothing is sent to a server or logged.

Details

The extension sends only the site you are on and, when your phone needs it, the username you typed earlier in the same tab (kept in memory for that tab).
