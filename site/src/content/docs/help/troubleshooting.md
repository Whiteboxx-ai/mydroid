## The Mac can't be found while pairing

- Check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network.
- Turn off VPNs for the first pairing.
- Allow **Local Network** for Mydroid: on the Mac, **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network**.

## "Mydroid can't be opened" on the Mac

Use the [install line](/get-started/install-mac/), which avoids the warning. If you downloaded the app by hand, use **Open Anyway** in Privacy & Security.

## Notifications don't show on the Mac

1. On the phone, open **Settings** in Mydroid and check **Notifications** is on.
2. If Android says the setting is restricted, [allow restricted settings](/get-started/install-android/#allow-restricted-settings).
3. [Keep Mydroid running](/help/keep-it-running/) on your phone.

## "Allow Mydroid on your Mac" stays on the phone

The phone is waiting for the Accessibility switch on your Mac.

1. On your Mac, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility** and switch **Mydroid** on.
2. Still waiting? Click **Reset and ask again** on the Mydroid card, or press **Show it on my Mac again** on the phone. macOS ties the switch to one exact copy of the app, so an updated or rebuilt Mydroid needs it again.

## It works for a while, then stops

Your phone is closing the app in the background. See [Keep it running](/help/keep-it-running/).

## A file only partly arrived

Send it again. Mydroid resumes where it stopped. Unfinished files wait in a hidden folder and appear in Downloads only when complete.

## The fill chip doesn't appear

Check the extension shows **Connected** in its toolbar popup, and that Mydroid is running on your Mac.

## Still stuck

[Open an issue](https://github.com/gautamm20/mydroid/issues/new/choose) with your phone, Android version and macOS version.
