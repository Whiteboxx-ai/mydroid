title: FAQ
description: Short answers to common questions.
---

**Does it cost anything?** No.

**Does it need an account or the internet?** No account. Your phone and Mac talk directly over your Wi-Fi. Bluetooth tells your phone when the Mac is near.

**Does anything go to a server?** No. Mydroid has no servers, no analytics and no crash reporting.

**Why isn't it on the App Store or Play Store?** It reads texts, call state and notifications, which stores restrict. It installs from GitHub instead, and every download has a checksum.

**Why does Android ask for "restricted settings"?** Android asks this for any app installed from a file. See [Install on your phone](/get-started/install-android/#allow-restricted-settings).

**Does it work with an iPhone?** No, Android only.

**Which Android versions?** 10 and newer. **Which macOS?** 14 and newer.

**Is the code open?** Yes, on [GitHub](https://github.com/gautamm20/mydroid).
