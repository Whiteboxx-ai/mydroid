## Remote

In the phone app, open **Remote** for a trackpad, keyboard, slide clicker and media controls. The first time, macOS asks you to allow Mydroid under **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

## Quick actions

Lock, Sleep, Ring and your own Shortcuts, one tap from the phone.

## Walk-away lock

Off by default. When on, your Mac locks after you walk away with your phone and wakes its screen when you return. It never unlocks your Mac: you still use Touch ID or your password. Turn it on in **Settings → Walk-away lock**; it needs Bluetooth on both devices.
