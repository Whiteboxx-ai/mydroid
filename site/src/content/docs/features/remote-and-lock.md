title: Remote and lock
description: Use the phone as a trackpad, a slide clicker, a music remote and a launcher for your Mac.
---

Open **Remote** on the phone. It has four tabs.

## Pad

The whole pad is a trackpad.

- **Tap** to click. **Two fingers** to scroll; tap with two fingers to right-click.
- **Three fingers** swipe between desktops and open Mission Control.
- **Hold, then move** to drag.
- The strip on the right scrolls. The two buttons at the bottom click.
- The keyboard button types on your Mac and shows what you typed. Modifier keys apply to the next key.
- Quick keys under the pad open Spotlight, switch apps, open Mission Control, copy, paste and take screenshots.
- The sliders icon sets pointer speed, tap-to-click and reverse scroll.

The first time, macOS asks you to allow Mydroid under **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. The phone waits and continues by itself.

## Present

Pick a deck from your recent files and tap **Play from start**. It opens and plays on your Mac. A Slides link opens in your Mac's browser.

While presenting you get big Back and Next buttons, a timer, a laser pointer, a black-screen button, and your volume keys change slides. For Keynote and PowerPoint the phone also shows the slide number, and Keynote's speaker notes. macOS asks once whether Mydroid may control the app.

## Playing

Shows what Music or Spotify is playing on your Mac, with the cover, a seek bar and volume. Other apps still answer the play, next and previous keys.

## Open

Search your Mac's apps and files, see what is open now, and open anything on your Mac with one tap. Files come from the folders you share in Mydroid for Mac.

## Quick actions and walk-away lock

Lock, Sleep, Ring and your Shortcuts are on the phone's home screen. **Walk-away lock** is off by default. When on, your Mac locks after you walk away with your phone and wakes its screen when you return. It never unlocks your Mac: you still use Touch ID or your password. Turn it on in **Settings → Walk-away lock**; it needs Bluetooth on both devices.
