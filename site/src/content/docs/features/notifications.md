---
title: Get Android notifications on your Mac
description: Mirror Android notifications over Wi-Fi, reply from your Mac when supported, and choose which apps appear.
---

Mydroid shows Android notifications on your Mac over Wi-Fi. Reply from the Mac when the Android app offers a reply action. You can also dismiss alerts from either device.

1. [Set up Mydroid](/get-started/) on both devices.
2. On your Mac, turn on **Notifications** in Mydroid Settings.
3. On Android, open Mydroid and allow **Notification access** when prompted.
4. Send yourself a test notification and check the Mydroid menu on your Mac.

![An Android Gmail notification in Mydroid for Mac](/screenshots/mac-notification.webp)

Mute an app in **Settings → Notifications** or from its banner. Quiet hours hide banners while keeping the notification list available.

## What gets mirrored?

Messaging apps share what appears in their notifications. For SMS conversations, use [Messages on your Mac](/features/messages-and-calls/#messages). Android may hide some sensitive notification content, including verification codes.

If Android blocks access, see [Allow restricted settings](/get-started/install-android/#allow-restricted-settings). If alerts stop when the phone locks, see [Keep Mydroid running](/help/keep-it-running/).
