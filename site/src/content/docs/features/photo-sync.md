---
title: Automatically sync Android photos to your Mac
description: Save new Android camera photos to your Mac over Wi-Fi and browse older photos on demand.
---

Mydroid copies new camera photos to **Pictures → Mydroid** while your phone and Mac are connected. You can also browse older photos and save the originals you want.

1. [Pair your phone](/get-started/pair/).
2. Turn on **Camera roll** in Mydroid on your Mac.
3. On Android, allow photo access. Choose all photos for automatic sync.
4. Take a new photo and check **Pictures → Mydroid** on your Mac.

![Browsing Android photos from the Mydroid Photos window on Mac](/screenshots/mac-photos.webp)

Open **Photos** in the Mydroid window to browse older photos. To add synced photos to Apple Photos, open **Settings → Photos** on your Mac. To move a video, [share it as a file](/features/file-transfer/).
