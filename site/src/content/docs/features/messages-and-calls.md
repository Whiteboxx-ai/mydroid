## Notifications

[Set up Android notifications](/features/notifications/) to see alerts, reply when supported, and dismiss them across both devices.

- **Mute an app** from its banner or in Settings → Notifications.
- **Quiet hours** hide banners; the list keeps everything.
- Banners from password managers and one-time codes aren't kept.

## Messages

Open **Mydroid** from the menu bar (or press ⌃⌥M) for the full window. **Messages** shows your SMS and app chats together. Turn on text messages in the window when it offers.

App chats show what arrived as notifications. Texts come from the phone on request.

## Calls

An incoming call shows the caller on your Mac. Answer, decline, mute or end it from there. You can also start a call from your Mac.

Call audio stays on your phone by default. To hear calls on your Mac, open **Mydroid → Settings → Calls** and choose **Set Up** under **Hear calls on your Mac**. This uses an existing Android Platform Tools installation on your Mac.

On your phone, open **Developer options → Wireless debugging**. Turn it on, then tap **Pair device with QR code** and scan the code on your Mac.
