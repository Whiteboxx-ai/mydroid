---
title: Clipboard and links
description: Copy on one device, paste on the other.
---

Clipboard sharing is **off by default**. Turn it on in **Settings → Clipboard** on your Mac.

- Text you copy on one device is available to paste on the other.
- Items that password managers mark as private are never sent.
- A link sent from your phone opens in your Mac's browser, or shows a banner with **Open** (your choice in Settings → General).

To send one thing on purpose, use **Share → Mydroid** or the **Paste** and **Text or link** buttons in the phone app. That works even with clipboard sharing off.

**Instant copy** (advanced, off by default) sends text the moment you tap Copy. It uses an Android accessibility service; some banking apps warn while it is on.
