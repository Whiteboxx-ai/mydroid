## Photos

New photos from your phone's camera roll arrive in **Pictures → Mydroid** on your Mac. Nothing is copied twice, and a dropped connection picks up where it stopped.

Browse the whole library in the Mydroid window under **Photos**, and save what you want. You can also add photos to Apple Photos in Settings → Photos.

To move a video, share it from your phone as a file.

## Send to Mac

- **Share → Mydroid** on the phone sends files, text and links to your Mac. Files land in **Downloads**.
- **Share → Quick Share → your Mac** works too.
- If your Mac is away, files wait on the phone for up to a day.

## Send to phone

Drop files or links on the Mydroid menu-bar popover, or use **Send to Phone** in a Mac app's Services menu.

## Mac folders on your phone

Your phone's Files app shows Desktop, Documents and Downloads from your Mac. Add or remove folders in **Settings → Files**. Saving to the Mac is off until you allow it per folder.
