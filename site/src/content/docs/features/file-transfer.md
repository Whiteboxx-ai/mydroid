---
title: Transfer files between Android and Mac over Wi-Fi
description: Send files, videos, and links between your Android phone and Mac without a USB cable or account.
---

Mydroid sends files between your Android phone and Mac over the same Wi-Fi. Use **Share → Mydroid** on the phone or drag a file onto the Mydroid menu on your Mac.

## Send a file to your Mac

1. [Pair your phone](/get-started/pair/).
2. On Android, open a file, photo, or video and tap **Share → Mydroid**.
3. Open **Downloads** on your Mac to find the file.

You can also receive files from Android’s **Quick Share** menu when it discovers your Mac on the local network.

![Mydroid for Mac showing received files and transfer sizes](/screenshots/mac-files.webp)

## Send a file to Android

1. Click Mydroid in the Mac menu bar.
2. Drag a file onto the Mydroid panel.
3. Open the received file from the notification on your phone.

To browse folders on your Mac from Android, choose what to share in **Mydroid → Settings → Files** on the Mac. Open **Mac files** in Mydroid on your phone.

For a camera roll that updates automatically, [set up photo sync](/features/photo-sync/).
