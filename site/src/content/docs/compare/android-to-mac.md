title: Ways to connect Android to a Mac
description: Compare Mydroid, LocalSend, KDE Connect, and DroidPort for file transfer, notifications, SMS, and remote controls.
---

Use **LocalSend** if your main job is sending a file. Try **Mydroid** if you also want notifications, SMS, camera photo sync, or a remote for your Mac. **KDE Connect** and **DroidPort** are worth a look if you already use the KDE Connect app on Android.

| App | Best fit | Setup and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mydroid** | Android notifications, SMS, camera photo sync, files, phone-as-trackpad controls, and phone-approved 2FA for Mac. | Free and open source. Install both apps and pair over local Wi-Fi. Standard call controls keep audio on the phone. [Setup](/get-started/) |
| **LocalSend** | Sending photos, documents, and other files across different operating systems. | Free and open source. Install on both devices and send over the local network. [LocalSend](https://localsend.org/) |
| **KDE Connect** | Sharing files, notifications, and remote controls across devices, especially if you already use KDE. | Free and open source. Check the macOS download and platform notes for the features you want. [KDE Connect](https://kdeconnect.kde.org/) |
| **DroidPort** | A native Mac window for SMS and MMS, notifications, photos, files, and clipboard. | One-time purchase. Uses KDE Connect on Android and works over the local network. [DroidPort](https://droidport.app/) |

## Is there a Phone Link app for Mac?

Microsoft’s Phone Link pairs a phone with a Windows PC. On a Mac, use a separate Android companion app such as Mydroid for notifications, SMS, and files. Check [how calls work](/features/messages-and-calls/#calls) if you need to talk through the Mac’s mic and speakers. [Microsoft Phone Link requirements](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/phonelink/phone-link-requirements-and-setup)
