Install Mydroid on both devices, scan the QR code on your Mac, then try sending a link from Android.

## Install both apps

1. [Install on your Mac](/get-started/install-mac/) and open Mydroid from the menu bar.
2. [Install on Android](/get-started/install-android/) and open Mydroid.
3. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi.

## Pair your phone

1. Tap **Scan** in Mydroid on Android.
2. Scan the QR code in the Mac setup window.
3. Check that both confirmation codes match, then confirm.
4. Choose what to sync.

![The Mydroid menu showing a phone connected to a Mac over Wi-Fi](/screenshots/mac-connected.webp)

## Try sending a link

1. Open a page in your phone’s browser.
2. Tap **Share → Mydroid**.
3. Look for the page in your Mac browser or the **Open** button in the Mydroid banner.

![An Android link arriving on a Mac with an Open button](/screenshots/mac-open-link.webp)
