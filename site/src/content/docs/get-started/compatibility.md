title: Mydroid compatibility and requirements
description: Check Android and macOS requirements, supported hardware, and what needs extra setup.
---

Mydroid requires **macOS 14 or later** and **Android 10 or later**. The Mac download supports Apple silicon and Intel. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi to pair.

| What you want to do | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Pair and send files | Both apps and a local Wi-Fi network where devices can reach each other. The in-app QR scanner uses Google Play services. |
| Mirror notifications | Android notification access. Sideloaded apps may require [Allow restricted settings](/get-started/install-android/#allow-restricted-settings). |
| Sync camera photos | Android photo access. Give access to all photos for automatic sync. |
| Send SMS or control cellular calls | An Android device that supports the feature and the relevant permissions. Audio stays on the phone in standard setup. |
| Hear calls on your Mac | A compatible phone, Android Platform Tools already installed on your Mac, and wireless debugging. [Call audio setup](/features/messages-and-calls/#calls) |
| Detect your Mac nearby | Android 12 or later and a phone with Bluetooth LE and Android companion device support. Ordinary sync uses Wi-Fi. |
| Use your phone as a trackpad | Accessibility permission for Mydroid on the Mac. [Remote setup](/features/remote-and-lock/) |
| Fill a sign-in code in your browser | A paired phone, Mydroid for Mac, and [Mydroid Authenticator](/features/authenticator/) in a supported Chromium browser. |

These are the supported requirements. Individual phone models and Android builds can handle background apps differently. If sync stops when the screen locks, [find the setting for your phone](/help/keep-it-running/).
