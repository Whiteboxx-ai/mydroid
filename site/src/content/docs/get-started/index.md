---
title: Set up Mydroid
description: Install Mydroid on your Mac and Android phone, then pair them over Wi-Fi.
---

You’ll need a Mac with macOS 14 or later and an Android 10 or later phone with Google Play services. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi.

1. [Install on your Mac](/get-started/install-mac/). Look for Mydroid in the menu bar.
2. [Install on Android](/get-started/install-android/). Download the APK on your phone and open it.
3. [Pair your phone](/get-started/pair/). Scan the Mac’s QR code and check that both devices show the same confirmation code.

## Check that it works

Open a link on Android. Tap **Share → Mydroid**. The link opens in your Mac browser or appears in a banner with **Open**.
